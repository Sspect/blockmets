SMP Server Rules

These rules may be adjusted as needed!

1. Respect All Players

No harassment, hate speech, or bullying. Treat everyone with kindness.

2. No Griefing

No griefing! Don’t destroy or steal from other players’ builds or property.
Staff can track block breaking and items taken from chests.

3. Protect the Environment

Don’t cause excessive damage to the landscape (e.g., avoid leaving floating trees).

4. Follow Staff & Appeal Process

Helpers and admins have the final say. Respect their decisions.
Not doing so may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal.
If you disagree with a decision, you can appeal to OMG Board members.

5. Report Issues

If you see someone breaking the rules, report it to a helper, admin, or board member.

6. No Cheating or Hacking

Do not use hacked clients, x-ray, duplication glitches, or any mods that give an unfair advantage.
Breaking this rule will result in a ban.

Allowed client-side mods:
  • Lightmatica
  • Simple autoclickers
Not allowed:
  • X-ray or ore-finding mods
  • Fly/Speed hacks
  • Anything that gives an unfair PvP advantage

7. How to Get Whitelisted

Add the check mark reaction to this post and type:

/whitelist add <MinecraftUserName>

Example: /whitelist add happygamer12

Other info

  • Server info: see website
  • Server address: survival.blockmets.no
  • Run Minecraft Java 26.2

Minecraft Spawn Project

This is the first time I’m trying something like this on the server, so we might adjust things as we go.

The idea is simple: react with an emoji to claim a small spawn project to build.

Each project should help players get a small starter amount of something they can then multiply themselves. For example:

  • A smithing template display with Armor Stands showing off the trims, while also letting players duplicate the templates they need.
  • A community crop field where players can harvest a small amount of seeds or crops and replant what they use.
  • A hatchery with Chicken, Sniffer, and Turtle Eggs that players can use to start their own populations.

The goal is not free unlimited resources at spawn, just enough to help players start their own supply.

Hopefully this also gives us a bunch of smaller spawn projects that different players can claim and build.
