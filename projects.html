Minecraft Spawn Project

This is the first time we are trying something like this on the server, so the details may change as we learn what works best.

Go to Discord to participate. The full project list and the emoji reactions are in the Discord post. Open it, choose a project, and react with its emoji to claim it.

Each project should provide a small starter amount of useful items that players can then multiply for themselves.

Claim it on Discord

Open the Discord post, choose a project, and react with its emoji.

Give a starter amount

Provide enough resources to help players begin, not an unlimited supply.

Build for the community

Create a useful, welcoming project that other players can understand and use.

Some exsamples of what to build

  • Smithing template display: show armor trims with Armor Stands and let players duplicate the templates they need.
  • Community crop field: let players harvest a small amount of seeds or crops and replant what they use.
  • Hatchery: provide Chicken, Sniffer, and Turtle Eggs so players can start their own populations.

The goal is to create a collection of smaller spawn projects that different players can claim and build while keeping resources sustainable. The website explains the concept; Discord is where you see the available projects and claim one.

Stronghold / End Portal Location

Overworld

404 ~ -1548

Nether

51 ~ -194

How to get there

  1. Use /warp spawn
  2. Enter the Nether, the portal at spawn goas to the nether roof
  3. Travel to around 51 ~ -194

To-do before the fight

  • Bring blocks for bridging and pillaring
  • Bring food
  • Bring bows and arrows
  • Bring water buckets
  • Bring good armor and weapons
  • Bring spare gear in case someone dies
  • Set your spawn in the Overworld near the Stronghold
  • Wearing a carved pumpkin prevents endermen from becoming aggressive when you look at them
