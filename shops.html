Shops

The Shops district is located on the Shopping Island. Use /warp shops to travel there. Build your shop in the designated area, respecting chunk spacing guidelines to create an organized and navigable shopping district.

Building Guidelines

1
Claim Your Space

You can claim as many chunks as you need for your shop. Just make sure your chunks are at least one chunk away from any other shop. If you want to be closer, ask the nearest shop owner for permission.

2
Viewing Chunks

To see chunk borders, press the following keys at the same time:

  • Windows: F3 + G
  • Mac: FN + F3 + G
3
Let People Know

Place a lectern with a book and quill in or near your shop to share:

  • Who built the shop
  • What it sells
